**This is a personal build.** It is self-signed and not notarized by Apple,
so macOS asks once before opening it (see Install).

Slouch Goblin 0.1.1 for Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 13 Ventura or later.

## What's new

- Fixed a freeze of the whole macOS menu bar after leaving Slouch Goblin
  running overnight. The app was replacing its menu several times a second,
  which eventually overwhelmed the system's menu bar process.
- Much lower CPU use while monitoring: about 18% of one core, down from
  30–50%. Pose detection now runs on the GPU, four times a second.
- The camera is released while the Mac or its display is asleep.
- If GPU pose detection ever crashes on a Mac, the app switches to the CPU
  for good on that Mac.

## Install

Download the `.dmg`, open it, and drag Slouch Goblin to Applications, replacing
the old copy if asked. Then:

1. Open Slouch Goblin from Applications. macOS says it cannot verify the app
   and offers only **Done** or **Move to Trash**. Choose **Done**.
2. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click
   **Open Anyway** next to the Slouch Goblin message. Confirm with your
   password.
3. Allow camera access if macOS asks.

You only do this once per version.

## Verifying your download

```bash
shasum -a 256 -c Slouch-Goblin-0.1.1-arm64.dmg.sha256
```
