Slouch Goblin 0.1.3 for Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 13 Ventura or later.

## What's new

- A fresh look for every window: setup, calibration, the live feed,
  Diagnostics and About.
- Setup explains what happens if macOS blocks the camera, and carries on by
  itself as soon as you allow it in System Settings.
- Calibration shows its progress in the capture button, and says plainly when
  the forward pose needs a little more lean.
- **Pause for 1 hour** pauses watching for an hour, and never past midnight.
  While paused, the window says when watching resumes.
- Reminders hold while someone else is in frame, and come back a few seconds
  after it's just you again.
- The menu always offers **Open Slouch Goblin**.
- The **Not hunching** button on a reminder now sits in the bottom-right corner
  of each display.
- If another app has the camera, or monitoring stops, the window says so
  plainly and offers what to do next.

## Install

Download the `.dmg`, open it, and drag Slouch Goblin to Applications, replacing
the old copy if asked. Then open it from Applications.

This build is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens without any security
warning.

## Verifying your download

```bash
shasum -a 256 -c Slouch-Goblin-0.1.3-arm64.dmg.sha256
```
